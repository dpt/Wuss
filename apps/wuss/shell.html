Wuss is a sort-of recreation of the RISC OS WindowManager (the Wimp). It's built on my DPTLib, a platform-independent C library shared across my projects. It exercises window creation, z-ordering, mouse routing and dirty-region redraw. It's compiled to WebAssembly via Emscripten's SDL3 port. It has a bunch of mini demo apps built into it.

Like in RISC OS you need a three-button mouse (buttons = SELECT/MENU/ADJUST). On a trackpad, swap MENU and ADJUST in the Configuring app. Click MENU over a window to open its menu. Click SELECT on the Apps icon on the icon bar to find the demo apps. Click a titlebar with SELECT to bring its window to the front. Drag a titlebar with SELECT to move a window, or with ADJUST to move it without raising it. Shift+F1 redraw all, Ctrl+Shift+F1 fill screen with random pixels (for testing redraw) Ctrl+=/Ctrl+- zoom in/out, Ctrl+0 actual size Shift+F3 pixel stress test The Shortcuts app lists every key.
